Module 9: Weight and Growth Tracking (ADG)

Measure calf and young-stock weights and average daily gain (grams per day)

📈

ADG formula (average daily gain):

ADG = ((current weight - previous weight) ÷ number of days) × 1000

A healthy-breed calf’s daily gain of 700–1,000 grams is considered ideal.

Target for beef calves
800g+ / days
